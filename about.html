Hello there!

Lots of little joys, made to order

Hi, we're the hands behind Whimsy Workshop! We make small handmade things — charms and trinkets that clip onto your bag, dangle from your phone, or sit on a shelf and make you smile.

No two are ever quite the same, so the one you pick is truly yours. You'll find us at markets around Windsor–LaSalle, or right here in the shop.
