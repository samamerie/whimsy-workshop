The maker

One pair of hands, a lot of little joys

Whimsy Workshop turns beads, yarn, clay and thread into everyday treasures — the kind that clip onto a bag, dangle from a phone, or sit on a shelf and make you smile.

Everything is made to order in small batches, so no two pieces are quite the same. Find us at markets around Windsor–LaSalle, or take a piece home from the shop.
